Figure 2. Composite overdose-vulnerability index for Dallas County census tracts.

Fill colour is a composite vulnerability score computed as the equal-weighted mean (0.20 each) of five min-max-normalised indicators: overdose mortality rate, poverty rate (ACS 2020-2024), uninsured rate (ACS 2020-2024), distance to the nearest naloxone access point, and inverted transit connectivity (DART stops within 0.5 mi). 643 tracts were ranked; 2 zero-population tracts were excluded (cross-hatched). Tracts are grouped into three Jenks natural-break tiers (Tier 3|2 cut = 0.338; Tier 2|1 cut = 0.434, both marked on the colour bar); the boundary of Tier 1 (highest vulnerability, 170 tracts) is outlined in heavy black. Fine dotted hatching marks the 592 tracts whose underlying death counts are CDC-suppressed (1-9); model-based rates are shown for these.

Tract boundaries: Census TIGER 2024. Projection: NAD83 / Texas North Central (EPSG:32138). CDC suppresses tract death counts of 1-9; 592 of 645 tracts (91.8%) carry suppressed counts. Model-based rates (4day-mt2f) are shown for all tracts; tract-level values should be read as smoothed estimates, not exact counts.
